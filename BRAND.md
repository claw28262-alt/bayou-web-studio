# Bayou Web Studio — Brand Guide

## Competitive Landscape (Houston)

### The Big Agencies ($5K-50K+ per project)
- **OWDT** — flashy, enterprise clients, way overpriced for small biz
- **Thrive Agency** — national chain with Houston office, cookie-cutter process
- **Agency Partner Interactive** — mid-market, $3K-10K projects

### The Budget Players ($500-2K)
- **Houston Web Design and Hosting** — Tomball-based, old school, targets NW Houston
- **Supfort** — been around since 2007, tries to do everything, generic
- **Fix Website Issues** — bottom of barrel, template mill

### The Gap (Where We Live)
Nobody is doing this well:
- Premium quality at an accessible price
- Actually local (not a national chain pretending to be Houston)
- Focused on small businesses that don't have websites at all
- Proactive outreach instead of waiting for clients to come to them
- Real business insights (competitive analysis, ad strategy) not just "here's a pretty page"

## Who We Are

**Bayou Web Studio** is a Houston-born web studio that builds websites for local businesses. We're not a big agency with a downtown office and bloated rates. We're a small, focused team that knows Houston and knows what local businesses actually need to grow online.

We started because we kept seeing great Houston businesses with no web presence at all. Restaurants, barbershops, auto shops, tattoo studios, salons, contractors. Places people love but can't find online. That felt like a problem worth solving.

## Who We Service

Local Houston businesses that either:
1. Have no website at all
2. Have an outdated site that hurts more than it helps
3. Want to show up when people search for their services

Our sweet spot: owner-operated businesses with 1-20 employees. The bakery on Westheimer. The mechanic in Montrose. The nail salon in Midtown. Real people running real businesses.

## Where We Come From

Houston. Not Austin, not Dallas, not a remote team pretending to be local. We live here, we eat here, we shop here. We know the neighborhoods because they're our neighborhoods.

## What Makes Us Different

1. **We show up with the work already done.** We don't pitch ideas. We build your site first, then show it to you. If you like it, it's yours. If not, no hard feelings.

2. **We include things other agencies charge extra for.** Competitive analysis of your local market. Google Ads strategy. SEO basics. That's not an upsell, that's the standard package.

3. **We keep it simple.** No contracts longer than a month. No hidden fees. No confusing packages with names like "Enterprise Gold Tier." You get a website, it looks good, it works, and people find you. That's it.

4. **We're business owners too.** We run businesses in Houston. We know what it's like to juggle everything. That's why we handle the web stuff so you don't have to think about it.

## Voice and Tone

- Professional but not corporate
- Confident but not salesy
- Direct. Say what you mean in as few words as possible.
- No buzzwords (synergy, leverage, ecosystem, disrupt)
- No double dashes
- No exclamation points in professional copy
- Write like a person, not a brochure
- If it sounds like ChatGPT wrote it, rewrite it

## Pricing Philosophy

Accessible but not cheap. We're not the $99 Fiverr special and we're not the $15K agency. We're the smart middle ground that makes business owners say "that's actually really fair."
